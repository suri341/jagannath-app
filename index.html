About Jagannath Enterprises

Jagannath Enterprises is a leading supplier of rice mill sortex machinery spare parts based in Kathipudi, Andhra Pradesh. We specialize in providing high-quality, reliable spare parts for rice processing equipment, including color sorters, sortex machines, and related machinery.

With years of experience in the industry, we have built a reputation for excellence in product quality, customer service, and technical expertise. Our commitment is to help rice mill owners and operators maintain peak performance of their equipment with genuine and compatible spare parts.

We serve rice mills across Andhra Pradesh and neighboring states, ensuring that quality spare parts are always available when you need them.
