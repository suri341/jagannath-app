About Jagannath Enterprises

Jagannath Enterprises is a leading provider of premium rice milling spares and comprehensive service support based in Kakinada, Andhra Pradesh. We specialize in delivering high-quality spare parts and expert service solutions for rice processing equipment, including rubber rolls, belts, sieves, bearings, sortex machines, and compressor systems.

With years of experience in the rice milling industry, we have built a reputation for excellence in product quality, technical expertise, and customer service. Our commitment is to help rice mill owners and operators maintain peak performance of their equipment through genuine spare parts and professional maintenance support.

We proudly serve rice mills across Andhra Pradesh and Telangana, providing reliable spare parts supply and on-site service support whenever you need it. Our team of experienced technicians ensures your milling operations run smoothly with minimal downtime.
